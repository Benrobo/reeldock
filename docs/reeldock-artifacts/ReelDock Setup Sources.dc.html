1 Setup · sources panel

Choosing between two cameras and three microphones

The panel today asks one question with two controls. Every device carries its own switch, which promises you can record both cameras at once — you can't, one camera and one microphone go into a recording — and the Selected pill answers the same question a second time. The list also grows with every device you own.

All three below share one model: role first, device second. Four fixed roles — phone, camera, microphone, phone sound — each with a single switch meaning "include this in the recording", and the device as a choice inside the role. Picking a device releases the previous one, because that is what actually happens.

1a Role cards, list unfolds in place

Four cards, always four. Press the device name and the alternatives unfold inside the card, so the choice never covers anything else. Picking one visibly releases the other.

New primitive, used by all three
Device picker

A recessed value row that reads as one answer, a chevron that says there are others, and a raised menu of rows with a checkmark column. It is the kit's popup‑button recipe extended with a second line of device detail and, for microphones, a live level in the row — so you can pick the mic that is actually hearing you.
